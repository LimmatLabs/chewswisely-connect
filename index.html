Chews Wisely – Connect Facebook Page

Admin setup tool for the app “Dog Food Social Leads API” (Limmat Labs GmbH). Customers never use this page.

What this app does

The Page admin connects our own Facebook Page “Chews Wisely Dog Food” once, on this page. After that, an automation running on our own server reads new comments on our posts, posts a short public reply as the Page, and sends one private reply (Messenger Private Replies) to people asking about our products. The same automation also handles comments and direct messages on the Instagram account linked to our Page. This page is only the one-time setup: login, permission grant, Page selection and webhook subscription.

1 Log in and grant permissions

The button below opens the Facebook Login dialog, where the Page admin grants our app the permissions listed here. After login, the status column shows which permissions were granted.

2 Select the Page

Uses pages_show_list (and business_management, because the Page belongs to a business portfolio) to list the Pages this admin manages: GET /me/accounts. Select “Chews Wisely Dog Food”.

3 Subscribe the Page to our webhook

Uses pages_manage_metadata: POST /{page-id}/subscribed_apps. This makes Facebook notify our automation whenever someone comments on a post of our Page. Without this subscription, new comments would not be detected.
